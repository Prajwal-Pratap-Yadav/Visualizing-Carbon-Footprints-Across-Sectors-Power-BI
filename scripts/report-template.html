Reporting trap
all-label sum / WORLD

Fourteen labels are not fourteen countries.

WORLD overlaps every region. France, Germany, Italy, Spain and UK also overlap EU27 & UK. Summing all labels inflates the total.

This ratio covers the whole fixed snapshot. The safe reconstruction uses Brazil, China, EU27 & UK, India, Japan, Russia, US and ROW exactly once, and checks them against WORLD for each day and sector.

Scope before interpretation

Source units are interpreted as Mt CO₂ from the preserved model's mega-ton label, the CO₂ dataset title and Carbon Monitor's public display; the uploader supplies no unit metadata. Six-sector totals are derived here, with no independent all-sector inventory total in the CSV. This is CO₂, with no GWP conversion or CO₂e claim. Land use lies outside the referenced fossil-fuel/cement scope; separate shipping treatment and international aviation allocation remain unverified.

The matching Kaggle v1 download is verified. Original Carbon Monitor release lineage remains unverified, and its current data has revisions and different geography coverage. Reconciliation tests arithmetic and overlap handling; it does not certify emission estimates.

Python re-creation, not a Power BI export. The preserved PBIX has no verified refresh/render in this environment.
